Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Culto Café we treat personal data with the same care we put into curating each coffee-shop listing. This document explains, as plainly as possible, what data we collect, what we use it for, and what rights you have over it, in line with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Spain's LOPDGDD.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Culto Café — Madrid, Spain
Contact: hola123@gmail.com

Culto Café is a project in its validation phase. Before operating with real customers, this section will be completed with the definitive legal entity, tax ID and registered address.

3. What we use your data for

  • Giving you coffee-shop recommendations through the WhatsApp guide, based on your taste.
  • Managing sign-up and the relationship with coffee shops that subscribe to a plan.
  • Answering your queries.
  • Measuring site usage and improving the service (analytics).
  • Showing relevant Culto Café ads on other platforms (if you accept advertising cookies).

4. Legal basis

We process your data based on your consent (when you message the guide, accept non-essential cookies, or fill in a form), the performance of the relationship you establish with us (e.g. signing up your coffee shop), and our legitimate interest in improving the service and communicating with you.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data while there is an active relationship (an ongoing conversation with the guide, your coffee shop's subscription) and afterwards for the legal limitation periods. You can request earlier deletion where the law allows it.

6. Who we share your data with

We don't sell your data. We only share it with the providers needed to run the service, acting as data processors:

  • Meta / WhatsApp Business Platform — so the guide can chat with you.
  • Google (Analytics, Tag Manager, Ads) — site analytics and campaign measurement.
  • Our hosting provider — to host the website.
  • Payment processor (once active) — to handle coffee shops' subscription payments; we never see or store your card details.

Some providers (Google, Meta) may process data outside the European Economic Area. In those cases, they rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or other safeguards recognised under data-protection law.

9. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability at any time by writing to hola123@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es) if you believe we haven't handled your data correctly.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this document to reflect legal or service changes. The last-updated date appears at the top of this page.
