About

A guide with judgement, not another search box

Culto Café is a discovery and loyalty platform for specialty coffee shops in Madrid. We connect people looking for a great coffee with the independent shops that genuinely do it well, through info verified shop by shop and a conversational guide.

The model is simple: people use it for free; coffee shops get visibility and loyalty tools closer to what the big chains have. We treat data with the same care we put into curating each listing.

FAQ

What people ask us most

What is Culto Café?

Culto Café is a directory of specialty coffee shops in Madrid (starting in Malasaña) with a conversational recommender on WhatsApp. It's free for people looking for coffee; coffee shops pay from €15/month to appear verified.

How much does it cost for my coffee shop to appear on Culto Café?

There are 4 plans: Free trial (€0/30 days), Presence (€15/month), Featured (€29/month) and Partner (€49/month). All include a verified listing on the site and the map; higher plans add priority in Culto's recommendations and extra visibility.

Do I need a card for the free trial?

No. The 30-day free trial doesn't ask for any payment details, just your coffee shop's sign-up.

How does Culto's WhatsApp recommender work?

You message Culto on WhatsApp describing what you're after — a quiet spot with wifi, a pet-friendly terrace — and it replies with verified coffee shops that match, instead of improvising like a generic search engine.

Can I cancel my plan whenever I want?

Yes. No paid plan has a minimum commitment: you can cancel any time.

Which cities is Culto Café available in?

We currently operate exclusively in Malasaña (Madrid). We will evaluate a future expansion to other neighborhoods and cities as the project grows.
